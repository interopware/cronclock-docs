# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 15, 2026**

Thank you for using **CronClock: Alarms & Reminders**. Your privacy is not an afterthought in this app — it's the design. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

**Short version: the app collects nothing, has no server, and keeps your data on your device. Backups go to your own iCloud Drive, which only you can access.**

## No data collection

CronClock does **not** collect any personal data. It does not ask you to create an account, and no account is required to use any feature.

## No network connections

The app makes **no network connections of its own** and has no server. It works fully offline. Your information is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. The only copies made off the device are the backups described below, which iOS stores in your own iCloud Drive.

## No third-party SDKs, ads, analytics, or trackers

The app contains **no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers**. There is no code in the app whose purpose is to observe, profile, or report on your behavior.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — your **alarms, reminders, medications, journal entries, and history logs** — is stored **on your device**. The only copies kept anywhere else are your backups (below). iCloud sync of the database is not used.

While an alarm or a reminder set to ring until stopped is ringing, its title is shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Medication names can be hidden from the Lock Screen in the app's Settings › Privacy.

## Backups, in your own iCloud

The app backs up your data so you don't lose it. Backups use **your own iCloud**, not any service run by the developer:

- **Automatic backups** are **on by default**. The app makes one at most every 6 hours, when something has changed. You can turn them off in Settings › Data & Backup › **Automatic backups**, and you can make one at any time with **Back Up Now**.

- **iCloud Drive backups** are written to a folder for this app in **your own iCloud Drive**, under your own Apple Account. If you use the app on more than one device, iCloud Drive carries those backups between your devices using **iOS's built-in iCloud mechanism** — the same one that syncs your photos and documents. Nothing goes through the developer: there is no developer server, and the app makes no network connection of its own. The folder is visible in the Files app, and you can delete any backup there at any time.
- **Exported files** are JSON files that **you** export to a location **you** choose.
- **Erase All Data** removes everything on the device. It does not touch iCloud: your backups stay in your iCloud Drive until you delete them.

Your iCloud data is protected by Apple's account security and iCloud encryption (including Advanced Data Protection, if you have turned it on). **Nobody but you can access these backups** — not the developer, and not any third party. **The developer never receives, stores, or has access to any of your data** — not your on-device data and not your backups.

## No account required

There is no sign-up and no login. You can use every feature of the app without providing any identifying information.

## System integrations

By design, the app does **not** use Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, App Intents, or activity donations. It does not share your alarms, reminders, or medications with other parts of the operating system. The app does use iOS notifications, because notifications are the only way it can alert you for alarms, reminders, and medication doses; you control whether notifications are allowed.

## Children's privacy

CronClock is safe for all ages and is rated **4+**. Because the app collects no personal data and has no server, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## Questions

If you have a question about this policy or your privacy, see the [Support](support) page for where to ask.
