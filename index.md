# CronClock: Alarms & Reminders

**A bedside display designed for nighttime use — power-efficient, sleep-friendly, and always readable.**

CronClock: Alarms & Reminders turns your iPhone or iPad into a calm, glare-free bedside clock and puts your alarms, reminders, medications, and a quick night journal in one place. It runs on your device — no account, no server, no tracking — so you get **peaceful, power-friendly nights** without giving anything away.

It's a **free app for iPhone and iPad**.

## Quick Summary

A quick, scannable look at what the app does.

### Bedside Clock (Night Mode)
- Full-screen clock: large dim text on a black background, made for a dark room.
- The time slowly **drifts** across the screen to prevent OLED burn-in.
- Status bar and home indicator are hidden for a clean, distraction-free face.
- Tap or long-press to reveal controls; they auto-hide after about 6 seconds.
- Works in any orientation and re-centers when you rotate the phone.
- Jot a quick journal note straight from the dark clock.

### Clock Customization
- Show/hide seconds, 12- or 24-hour time, show/hide the date.
- Brightness from 15% to 100%.
- 12 text colors ranked by OLED power use (deep red is the default and easiest on the battery; blue uses the most power and is the most sleep-disrupting), plus a custom color picker.
- 18 font families, size 60–220 pt, weight and italic, and drift tuning — with a live preview. **Use Lowest-Power Motion** sets the slowest drift and the longest wait in one tap (it does not change color or brightness).

### Alarms
- Time, name, and schedules: Once, Every day, Weekly by weekday, Monthly, Annually, or Custom dates.
- Two delivery switches: **Ring as a full-screen alarm** (rings until you stop it, even in silent mode) and **Send a notification** (a banner and sound that follow silent mode). Each has its own tone.
- 7 gentle built-in tones (for example, "Dawn" rises slowly), plus "Default" (the iOS sound) and "None"; or **Import a Sound…** of your own.
- **Quick Set** for a new alarm, **Set Time from Now…** on an existing one.
- Enable or disable each alarm; delete one from its editor (**Delete Alarm**, which asks first).

### Reminders
- Three ways to schedule: **Once**, **Repeats** (calendar rules), or **Chosen dates**.
- Four types: **Don't notify** (on your list, no notification), **Notify once** (one notification, no snooze), **Keep until done** (reminds again until you mark it done), and **Log by deadline** (needs a note attached before the deadline).
- Group related reminders into **Categories** (like "Household"), with a month calendar, Upcoming lists of what is due now, what is next, and recent history, and adherence reports.
- Color coding, 7 built-in tones plus custom audio, and "Ring until stopped" for items that must not be missed.

### Medications
- Dose reminders with frequency shortcuts and **timing rules** (Regular, Strict timing, or Only when needed). Each dose is graded **on time** (within 30 minutes), **close** (within an hour), **late**, or **missed** (not taken by halfway to the next dose).
- For each dose: **Dose taken** (records the exact time), **Dose not taken**, **Snooze**, or **Silence**.
- A floating dose **bubble** drifts over the app when a dose is due — tap it to jump straight there.
- An adherence report (percent and counts) and a color tag per medication. Dose alerts are Time Sensitive, so they can break through Focus.

### Journal
- Quick bedside notes grouped by night, newest first.
- Capture straight from Night Mode.
- Tags (filter by tag, manage tags) and full-text search that shows the matching line; edit or delete anytime.

### Privacy
- Optionally hide medication names on the Lock Screen (Settings › Privacy).
- No Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, App Intents, or activity donations — everything stays on-device.

## Privacy at a glance

- **No server.** The app makes no network connections of its own.
- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for.
- **No tracking, ads, or analytics.** There are no third-party SDKs.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Backups go to *your own* iCloud Drive — automatic backups are on by default and can be turned off — or to a JSON file *you* export and control. The developer never receives any of it.
